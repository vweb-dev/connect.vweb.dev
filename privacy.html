TRANSPARENCY

Privacy & Data Use.

This page describes how VWEB.DEV Connect has been designed to process information. Operational practices should be reviewed and confirmed before public launch.

Publisher thumbnails and on-device QR tools

Course cards can load thumbnail images directly from YouTube's image CDN, even before you start playback. Embedded videos only load when selected. QR codes for Wi-Fi details, contact information, WhatsApp messages and links are processed locally; QR data is not submitted to our web server. Downloaded QR images may contain private information, so store and share them carefully.

Academy and My Workspace data

Curated course notes, manually marked lesson progress, saved apps, and recent visited sections are stored locally in this browser. They are not automatically sent to VWEB.DEV Connect or the ISP database. You may export, import, or clear them in My Workspace. Exported JSON can contain private study notes. YouTube embedded players and official external software websites collect data under their own policies when opened.

Convert Studio and local files

The image, PDF, audio and video conversion tools process selected files locally in the browser. Files are not transmitted to our website API as part of conversion. PDF Studio downloads the open-source pdf-lib JavaScript library from a third-party CDN on first use; the library code loads into your browser but your selected files are not uploaded to the CDN. The VERT advanced-conversion link leaves this service and is governed by its own policies. Browser memory may temporarily hold conversion results until you clear the tool or close the tab.

Free visitor tools

Local image, text, conversion and password tools run in your browser. Files and tool content are not uploaded to the VWEB.DEV Connect backend by these utilities. The website host/CDN may maintain ordinary server access and security logs.

Optional Connect accounts and support

If you open My Account and register, we retain your submitted name, email, secured password hash, optional marketing preference, merchant submissions, and support requests. Anonymous homepage visits do not initiate account sessions or database reads. Support requests are accessible only to authenticated users and authorized administrators.

Retired Internet and Rewards features

My Internet, PPPoE account linking, hotspot vouchers and learning reward points are no longer offered by VWEB.DEV Connect. Legacy personal records may still remain in previously deployed databases until reviewed and deleted under the operator's applicable retention obligations. Their APIs no longer allow normal public access. Contact support about historical records, if applicable.

VWEB Entertainment and browser games

On-demand podcasts, games and learning resources load external content directly from providers such as YouTube, Spotify, Open Trivia DB and Open Library. Providers may process your network address, watch/listening activity or cookies under their own terms. We avoid loading streaming players until you click Play. Bookmarks, courses and local notes are stored in browser local storage and can be cleared with site data. The application does not relay or host the actual audio/video. Browser quizzes and puzzles score your answers locally and do not earn monetary or internet usage rewards.

Third-party AI and external links

Puter AI runs through the external Puter service. Your prompts and generated content are subject to its own service terms, account login, credit allowances and privacy arrangements. External merchants and news sites operate their own websites and policies. Avoid submitting sensitive business, customer or private network information into AI tools.

Security and retention

Account sessions are protected by secure cookies and private dashboard data requires authentication. Actual retention, backups, any historical retired subscriber records, and deletion requests must be reviewed by the operator before production launch.
