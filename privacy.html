TRANSPARENCY

Privacy & Data Use.

This page describes how VWEB.DEV Connect has been designed to process information. Operational practices should be reviewed and confirmed before public launch.

Convert Studio and local files

The image, PDF, audio and video conversion tools process selected files locally in the browser. Files are not transmitted to our website API as part of conversion. PDF Studio downloads the open-source pdf-lib JavaScript library from a third-party CDN on first use; the library code loads into your browser but your selected files are not uploaded to the CDN. The VERT advanced-conversion link leaves this service and is governed by its own policies. Browser memory may temporarily hold conversion results until you clear the tool or close the tab.

Free visitor tools

Local image, text, conversion and password tools run in your browser. Files and tool content are not uploaded to the VWEB.DEV Connect backend by these utilities. The website host/CDN may maintain ordinary server access and security logs.

Accounts and ISP-linked data

If you register, the application stores the name, email, salted password hash and account preferences you submit. A PPPoE username request remains pending until authorized ISP staff independently verify account ownership. Only then can data from the ISP's authorized accounting system be displayed to the matching signed-in account. No PPPoE password or home router administrator password is requested in the PWA.

Optional rewards and support

Learning task completions, points and pending voucher requests are stored on the server. Points do not represent usable internet until an ISP-authorized guest gateway issues a voucher. Merchant submissions and support requests are reviewed by authorized admins.

Notifications and promotions

Promotional messaging is optional and requires separate consent. Browser Web Push also requires notification permission. You can disable notifications in browser settings and in your Connect account. First-party sponsored placements may count approximate outbound clicks; these are not proof of a sale. Some outbound links may be affiliate relationships and should be clearly marked.

VWEB Entertainment and browser games

TV, radio, podcasts, games and learning resources load external content directly from providers such as YouTube, Spotify, Radio Browser, Open Trivia DB and Open Library. Providers may process your network address, watch/listening activity or cookies under their own terms. We avoid loading streaming players until you click Play. TV/radio favorites are stored in your browser local storage and can be cleared with site data. The application does not relay or host the actual audio/video. Browser quizzes and puzzles score your answers locally and do not change an ISP internet plan or quota.

Third-party AI and external links

Puter AI runs through the external Puter service. Your prompts and generated content are subject to its own service terms, account login, credit allowances and privacy arrangements. External merchants and news sites operate their own websites and policies. Avoid submitting sensitive business, customer or private network information into AI tools.

Security and retention

Account sessions are protected by secure cookies and private dashboard data requires authentication. Actual data retention, backups, ISP contractual roles, deletion requests and any legal notices must be reviewed by the operator before production launch. Public Wi-Fi traffic may be managed and logged by the authorized ISP gateway according to applicable requirements.
