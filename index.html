01 / ACTIVATION METHODS

Understand the four methods

Method availability, supported editions and behavior vary. Consult the maintained upstream documentation for current compatibility.

View upstream method list ↗
01WINDOWS

HWID

Windows-focused digital license activation approach documented by MAS. Check your Windows edition and license rights rather than assuming compatibility.

Digital licensing · Windows
02OFFICE

Ohook

Office-oriented activation approach. It is unrelated to signing in with a licensed Microsoft 365 account and is not Microsoft-supported.

Office applications
03WINDOWS + OFFICE

TSforge

An activation method documented for certain Windows and Office products. Consult MAS compatibility notes for specific product versions and limitations.

Version-dependent support
04KMS-BASED

Online KMS

Uses a KMS-style activation workflow. Legitimate Microsoft KMS licensing is intended for eligible organizational volume-license environments; third-party servers carry licensing and trust risks.

Organizational licensing context

INSTALL ON IPHONE OR IPAD
VWEB.DEV ConnectHome Screen web app · No App Store required
  1. Open Connect in Safari.Use Safari on your iPhone or iPad. Other browsers may show a different menu.
  2. Tap Share □↑.It is usually at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”.You may need to scroll the Share options. Enable “Open as Web App” when shown.
  4. Tap Add.Launch Connect using the new Home Screen icon.

On newer iOS versions, Safari may offer Add to Home Screen under its menu. Installation is completed by you in Safari, not by a website pop-up.
